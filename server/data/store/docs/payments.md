# Payments

We accept UPI, all major debit and credit cards, net banking, and cash. Cash on delivery is available for orders up to 10,000 rupees within Bengaluru.

No-cost EMI is available on credit cards for appliance purchases above 5,000 rupees, over 3 or 6 months, depending on the bank. Ask at checkout and we will tell you which banks are eligible.

A GST invoice is provided with every order. If you need the invoice in a business name with a GST number, tell us at the time of purchase and we will add it. We do not store card details; card payments are handled by the payment gateway.
