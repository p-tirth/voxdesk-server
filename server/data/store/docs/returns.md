# Returns and exchanges

You can return most items within 7 days of delivery for a refund or exchange. The item must be unused, in its original packaging, and you must have the receipt or the order number. Bring it to the Indiranagar store or arrange a pickup by calling the support line.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days after we receive and inspect the item. Cash-on-delivery orders are refunded by bank transfer, so we will ask for your account details.

Some items cannot be returned once opened for hygiene or safety reasons: this includes personal-care items and any item marked non-returnable on the receipt. Small appliances can be returned if they are unused; if an appliance is defective, it is handled under warranty rather than the return policy.

Exchanges for a different size, colour, or model are free within the 7-day window as long as stock is available; if there is a price difference you pay or are refunded the difference.
