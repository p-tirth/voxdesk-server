# Shipping and delivery

We deliver across Bengaluru. Orders within the city are usually delivered in 2 to 4 business days. Areas farther out, such as Whitefield, Electronic City, and Kengeri, may take an extra day or two.

Delivery is free on orders above 999 rupees. Below that, a flat delivery charge of 49 rupees applies. Bulky items like large appliances are always delivered free within the city.

You can also choose store pickup at Indiranagar at no charge; we hold a ready order for 3 days. We do not currently ship outside Bengaluru or offer same-day delivery. For an existing order, ask for its status by order number and we will share the latest update and the expected delivery date.
