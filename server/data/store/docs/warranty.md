# Warranty

All small appliances — air fryers, kettles, mixer grinders, hand blenders, and coffee makers — carry a one-year manufacturer warranty from the date of purchase. The warranty covers manufacturing defects and motor or heating-element failure under normal home use.

To make a warranty claim, bring the appliance and your receipt or order number to the Indiranagar store, or call the support line to book a pickup. We send it to the brand's authorised service centre; a repair or replacement usually takes 7 to 14 days. Loan units are not provided while a claim is being processed.

The warranty does not cover physical damage, water damage from misuse, normal wear of accessories, or problems caused by voltage fluctuation when a stabiliser was not used. Kitchenware and home goods such as pans, knives, towels, and clocks are not covered by a manufacturer warranty, but if they arrive damaged or defective you can report it within the 7-day return window.
